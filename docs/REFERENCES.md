## Rejecting

- Draggable polaroids (Aceternity): polaroids stay put, hover only.
- Zandani approach cards and stat row: approach becomes a Notepad essay window instead.
- Zandani two-button contact with no form.
- Magic UI full-circle orbit: the semicircle fan wins.
- Kisimoff neon circuit-board boot visuals: we keep only the typed diagnostic boot.
- Anything drifting toward brutalistwebsites.com (the outlier): if it looks stripped, the warmth is gone.
